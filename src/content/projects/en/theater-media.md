**The challenge:** take creative ideas all the way from **scriptwriting** through production, **directing**, **performing**, editing and distribution to **final delivery** as a **live performance** in front of an audience.

I developed **two full-cycle theatre productions**, from initial concept to live performance, both currently staged at **several major venues across Madrid**. The process began with **script and concept development**.

Once the scripts were final, I managed the **entire pre-production phase**, coordinating **casting, shoots, rehearsals, set and stage design, wardrobe** and **promotional planning**. The approach balanced **artistic creativity** with **logistical precision**, keeping creative departments, technical crews and marketing teams aligned. I also developed the **entire creative process of the teasers** myself.

During production, I oversaw **rehearsals and scene direction** to keep standards high and meet the schedule. Working hands-on with the **lighting, sound and stage management teams** was central to delivering a **cohesive, immersive audience experience**.

Post-production focused on **promoting the shows** with cohesive **visual and video materials** for **social media and press**, so that every piece of external communication reflected each show's **creative identity and emotional impact**.

To take the shows beyond Madrid, I also **designed and created the shows' dossiers**, bringing together synopsis, creative team, visuals and technical needs, and used them to **pitch and distribute the productions to theatres across Spain**.

**Result:** *Esta Es Mi Parada*, which featured **live music**, was a **success**, with **positive audience feedback** and **continued runs in notable Madrid theatres**. *Fracasada Rumbo al Caribe* is **currently on stage**. Both show how **strategic storytelling**, **collaboration** and **structured production workflows** can turn creative ideas into **impactful live experiences**.

### Key learnings

- Managed **full-cycle productions** from concept to live execution and promotion.
- Unified **multidisciplinary teams** under a **shared creative and strategic vision**.
- Strengthened **storytelling and brand expression** through narrative, performance and design.
- Integrated **AI tools** to enhance the **brainstorming process**.
- Created **emotionally resonant, audience-focused performances** to boost engagement.

### Key challenges

- **Balancing creativity with logistics:** keeping my creative vision intact while managing **complex timelines and resources**.
- **Cross-team coordination:** ensuring smooth communication between **artistic and technical teams and the theatres** through changing production phases.
- **Adapting to change:** handling creative revisions, new directions and **real-time performance adjustments** with **agile, flexible workflows**.
