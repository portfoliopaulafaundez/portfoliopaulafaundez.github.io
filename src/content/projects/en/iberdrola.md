**The challenge:** improve an existing app that people were using but that **wasn't delivering the expected results**. The design had to follow the **brand book**, and most of the creative elements were already in place, so the task was to **rethink the architecture** and create a **new design that worked**: **more intuitive** and **more accessible**.

I contributed to the **design and creative development** of an **intuitive, cross-platform app** (Android, iOS and desktop) that helps **electric vehicle drivers** easily find available **charging stations**. As part of the **UX design team** and as **copywriter**, I shaped a **clear and engaging product narrative**, making sure the app's functionality, content and interface worked together to deliver a **seamless user experience**.

Throughout the project, I worked closely with **designers, developers and stakeholders** to refine **user flows** and **visual storytelling**. Using **design thinking**, we expanded the original MVP with new features, including **real-time, location-based search** for nearby charging stations, which **significantly improved accessibility and user satisfaction**.

**Result:** just right after its launch, it had a strong engagement, with a **4.6/5 App Store rating** from **11K+ reviews** and **over 100K downloads** on Google Play, proof of both **design impact** and **storytelling effectiveness**.

### Key learnings

- Discovered how **structured analysis strengthens storytelling** and user experience.
- Learned to create **intuitive, accessible content** through **clear organisation and naming**.
- Saw the value of proposing **creative, out-of-scope ideas** to enhance the final product.
- Gained experience **collaborating across disciplines** to deliver **cohesive outcomes**.

### Key challenges

- Balancing **creativity** with the **brand constraints** of a predefined UI kit.
- Aligning **client-provided assets** with an **evolving creative direction**.
- Managing **feedback** while keeping a **consistent narrative and visual vision**.
