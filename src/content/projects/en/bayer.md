**The challenge:** own Supradyn's **digital campaign end to end**, from the first brief to **final execution**, while keeping **every team aligned** behind **one digital strategy**. The website content had to be updated in step with the **social media and news calendar**, and every **experiential activation** had to tell the same story as the digital channels.

I turned **client briefs** into **clear, actionable plans** for the creative and technical teams, so everyone knew what was needed, by when and why. From there, I led **cross-functional collaboration** between **design, content and development**, keeping the campaign's **quality and brand consistency** intact across every update.

I kept the website **in step with the social media and news calendar** and drove **on-time delivery** across **all digital touchpoints**. I also worked closely with the client to make sure **experiential activations** were aligned with the **digital strategy**, so the brand spoke with **one voice online and offline**.

**Result:** Supradyn is now available in **most pharmacies across Spain** and **stands out against its competitors**.

### Key learnings

- **Turning briefs into plans:** translating client goals into **clear, actionable steps** that creative and technical teams can deliver on.
- **Cross-functional leadership:** aligning **design, content and development** around a single campaign vision.
- **Integrated campaigns:** connecting **digital and experiential** touchpoints so they reinforce each other.

### Key challenges

- **Keeping pace with the calendar:** updating the website in step with **social media and news** without compromising quality.
- **Team alignment:** keeping **multiple teams and stakeholders** working toward **one digital strategy**.
- **Brand consistency:** maintaining a **consistent message** across web, social and live activations.
