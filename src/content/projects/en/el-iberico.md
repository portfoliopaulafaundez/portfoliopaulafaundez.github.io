**The challenge:** El Ibérico had a **stable relationship with its readers**, but it wasn't creating the kind of **creative material** that could **attract new ones**.

So I went, quite literally, **to the streets of London** to find the **most iconic outfits** and capture their **modern, chic vibe**. I turned that into a **fashion film**, *Street Style London*, which I **conceived, shot and edited entirely myself**, giving the newspaper a fresh, visual way to connect with its audience.

**Result:** the film caught readers' attention, and the newspaper has kept **creating new content** to **retain the readers it won** with it.

### Key learnings

- **Reaching new audiences:** seeing how **visual, creative content** can open up an established publication to **new readers**.
- **Owning the whole process:** taking a piece from **idea and street casting** to **shooting and final edit** on my own.
- **Capturing a mood:** translating a city's **style and attitude** into a short film with a clear **modern, chic** identity.

### Key challenges

- **Finding the story on the street:** spotting **iconic outfits** in real time and turning them into a **cohesive narrative**.
- **Working solo:** handling **concept, filming and editing** single-handedly while keeping a **professional finish**.
- **Fitting the brand:** creating something **new and stylish** that still felt right for the newspaper's **existing readers**.
