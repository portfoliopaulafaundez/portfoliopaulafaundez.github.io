**The challenge:** redesign Best Arctic's existing website. Once the client approved our proposal, I led the **planning and coordination** with **cross-functional teams** to bring the project to life.

Over a **six-month timeline**, I built a **detailed production schedule** and applied **agile methodologies** to ensure **on-time, high-quality deliverables**. From the early concept and redesign stages through development and content execution, I coordinated **multidisciplinary teams** of designers, developers and content creators to align **creative vision and functionality**.

The goal was to refresh the site with a more **user-friendly and visually engaging experience**. The new site is significantly **more accessible** and **easier to manage**, with an intuitive interface that improved the user experience and **directly contributed to higher sales**.

Throughout the project, I focused on **clear communication**, **streamlined workflows** and **creative storytelling**, so the final product not only met the client's expectations but also **strengthened their digital brand presence**.

**Result:** the new site **significantly boosted traffic growth**, with a **direct impact on sales**, and the brand's **presence in AI tools** has grown as well.

### Key learnings

- **Cross-team coordination:** aligning creative, technical and content teams under a shared vision, and managing and supervising **teams of more than 10 people**.
- **Agile project management:** running iterative processes, setting **clear milestones** and **adapting quickly** to changing priorities.
- **Creative storytelling:** seeing how strategic storytelling and visual communication **directly influence user experience and conversion**.
- **AI & automation:** exploring how **AI tools** can support faster content iteration and creative brainstorming, an approach I still apply in production workflows.
- **User-centric thinking:** designing every creative element around the **end-user journey**, with a focus on **accessibility and clarity**.

### Key challenges

- **Balancing creativity with functionality:** keeping the creative vision strong while meeting **technical and UX constraints** took constant alignment and flexibility.
- **Complex timeline management:** coordinating **multiple deliverables and stakeholders** within a tight six-month schedule taught me to **prioritise under pressure**.
- **Brand consistency:** with many contributors, keeping tone and visual identity consistent required **clear communication and precise documentation**.
